# Payments engine (Phase B)

_How money moves through Vendly from the Oct 2026 build. Code lives in `Back-end/src/modules/payments/`._

## The customer's card

- Customers save a card once: `POST /payments/setup-intent` returns a Stripe SetupIntent client secret for the Stripe card form; the card is stored on the customer's Stripe profile (`User.billing_id`).
- `GET /payments/methods`, `PATCH /payments/methods/:id/default`, `DELETE /payments/methods/:id` (refused while an active booking uses the card).
- A booking request carries `payment_method_id` (the chosen saved card). Nothing is charged at request time.

## Payment plans

Vendors pick a plan per package (`VendorListing.payment_plan`); admins can set a category default (`ServiceCategory.default_payment_plan`); otherwise **full on accept**. The marketplace returns `effective_payment_plan` on every listing.

| Plan | What happens |
|---|---|
| `FULL_ON_ACCEPT` | Price + Vendly Protection fee charged when the vendor accepts. |
| `DEPOSIT_50` | 50% + fee on accept; balance 7 days before the event. |
| `DEPOSIT_25` | 25% + fee on accept; balance 14 days before the event. |
| `PAY_AFTER_SERVICE` | Card authorized (not captured) when the event starts; captured when the vendor marks the job complete. |
| `DEPOSIT_CUSTOM` | The package's own deposit (`deposit_percent`, 10–90%) + fee on accept; the balance `balance_due_days` (1–60) before the event, or with `balance_after_service` after the service (below). The numbers are copied onto the booking when the vendor accepts. Never a category default. |

Rules in `payment-schedule.ts` (unit-tested): the fee always rides on the first charge; a balance that would already be overdue (event sooner than the lead time) is merged into the first charge; deposit plans without an event date fall back to full on accept.

**Balance after the service** (`DEPOSIT_CUSTOM` with `balance_after_service`, owner's ask Nov 2026): the balance installment has `after_service = true` and is never charged on its date (shown as due at the event). The vendor must upload photo proof before marking the booking complete. The balance is charged when the customer confirms the work (`POST /bookings/:id/approve`), or, if they neither confirm nor report a problem, when the release time comes (48 hours after completion, `runReleases`), just before the vendor is paid. An open problem report holds it; a booking refunded in full or cancelled never charges it. A failed balance gets the usual 3 tries over 48 hours (no 12-hour deadline: the booking already happened) and is paid out to the vendor once paid (`payOutLateInstallments`). The deposit follows the deposit-plan rules below.

## Charging

- When the vendor accepts (`confirm`), the plan is snapshotted into `booking_installments` and anything due now is charged off-session (`PaymentsService.chargeInstallment`). Funds stay on the platform balance under `transfer_group: booking_<id>`.
- A cron every 10 minutes charges installments as they fall due and retries failures.
- Retry policy (full on accept, pay after service, add-ons, and deposit plans booked before Oct 2026): 3 attempts, 24h apart. Each failure emails and pushes the customer. After the third failure (or a bank that insists on 3-D Secure, or a booking with no saved card) the installment is `FAILED` and the customer retries from the app: `POST /bookings/:id/installments/:iid/retry` with an optional new `payment_method_id` (resets the attempt counter, only while the row is as the customer saw it). A retry on a cancelled, declined or fully refunded booking is refused. A retry racing the sweep or the automatic retry is refused with 409 "A payment is already going through." (never a second charge); the apps show it as in progress and reload.
- The sweep charges the oldest due first; every attempt moves its row on (charged, retry later, or waiting for the customer), so nothing can hold up the batch.
- A pay-after-service charge that falls due once the job is already complete is charged outright (no hold); a hold that lands while the job is being completed is captured straight away.
- Hosted Checkout (`POST /bookings/:id/checkout`) is only for bookings without installments; a booking on a payment schedule is refused (it would be charged twice).
- `Booking.paid_at` is set on the first successful charge, so vendors can start work once the deposit is in.
- Stripe webhooks (`payment_intent.succeeded / payment_failed / canceled / amount_capturable_updated`) reconcile installments by `metadata.installment_id`, so a timed-out API call never leaves a paid installment unrecorded.

## Missed deposit or balance (deposit plans, owner's rules Oct 2026)

`payments/payment-due.ts`, `booking/payment-deadlines.service.ts`. Applies to `DEPOSIT_50` / `DEPOSIT_25` / `DEPOSIT_CUSTOM` bookings made from this release (`Booking.deposit_rules`; the migration set it to false on older bookings, which keep the 48-hour retries).

- The deposit is the plan's first charge (or, when the event was too close to split it, the full payment that includes it); the balance is the second.
- Any failure of either (declined, no saved card, the bank needs the customer) starts a **12-hour** deadline (**48 hours** on large bookings, below) (`Booking.payment_due_by`, `payment_due_kind` = deposit | balance), set once by the first failure; later failures don't move it.
- The booking stays `CONFIRMED` / `IN_PROGRESS`; the API adds `payment_state` (`pending_deposit` | `pending_balance`) and `payment_state_label` to bookings and `payment_state` / `payment_due_by` to the payments timeline. Every booking list and page (web, app, admin) shows **Pending deposit / Pending balance** instead of the status.
- One automatic retry about 6 hours after the first failure (none when the bank needs the customer, or when it would land within an hour of the deadline). The customer can pay any time before then with the saved card or another card (`POST /bookings/:id/installments/:iid/retry`, as before).
- The customer is emailed and pushed straight away (amount, the deadline in the time zone the booking was made in, a pay link `…/bookings/:id?pay=1`) and once more with about 2 hours left (`payment_due_reminded_at`). The vendor is told once that the payment is pending and when the deadline is.
- Paid in time: the pending state clears (`markPaid`). A date amendment that moves a pending balance's due date later clears it too, and the balance is charged on the new date.
- Still unpaid at the deadline: a job every 10 minutes (job lock `payment-deadlines`) cancels the booking for non-payment: `cancelled_by = SYSTEM`, `cancel_reason_code = non_payment`, claimed only while the deadline has passed, so a rerun or a payment that landed meanwhile never cancels twice. A charge still going through at Stripe waits for the next run; a booking with an open dispute is left for Vendly. Both are left out of the job's query (not skipped inside it), so however many there are they never fill the batch. It settles like a customer cancellation (below). Both sides are emailed and pushed.

## Large bookings, the fee cap and bank payments (owner, Nov 2026)

`platform/fee-config.ts`, `payments/bank-payments.ts`, `payments/payment-due.ts`. Migration `20261104000000_large_payments`.

- **Fee cap**: the Vendly Protection fee is never more than Admin → Settings `customer_service_fee_maximum` ($500 by default; saved empty = no cap), category fees included. Applied wherever a fee is worked out (booking preview and request, quotes, amendments, suggested changes, rentals); add-ons sent later only fill what's left under the cap for the booking. Existing bookings keep their fee. `GET /payment-settings` (public) gives the apps the percent, minimum, cap and the large booking rules for their wording ("5%, up to $500").
- **Large booking**: total (price + fee) at or above `large_payment_threshold` ($5,000 by default; 0 turns it off). Deposit plans then get **48 hours** to pay a missed deposit or balance (one retry about 24 hours in, the reminder with about 6 hours left). The booking preview and the payments timeline return `large`, `payment_due_hours` and `bank_allowed`. The deposit clause says "within 12 hours of failing (48 hours on bookings of $5,000 or more)" (merge field `large_payment_threshold`); `ensureDefaultTemplates` publishes the next version of each active template once, in place; signed contracts keep their text.
- **Bank payments (ACH Direct Debit)**: only on large bookings without a card hold (not pay after service, not rentals with a security deposit). Saved with `POST /payments/setup-intent { type: 'us_bank_account' }` (instant verification through Stripe Financial Connections; the customer accepts the mandate when confirming) and listed with cards by `GET /payments/methods?bank=1`. `Booking.payment_method_type` says the booking pays from a bank account.
- Each charge is an off-session PaymentIntent on the bank account. Stripe answers `processing`: the installment stays `PROCESSING` with `clearing_since` set ("Processing" in the apps, pending in the transaction history). It isn't paid (no receipt, nothing unlocked, no `paid_at`) and isn't failed (no retries; the deadline job never cancels a booking while it's on its way). `payment_intent.succeeded` marks it paid as for cards; `payment_intent.payment_failed` while clearing is handled like a declined card without the automatic retry (deposit plans: the usual window, 48 hours, to pay another way).
- A bank payment returned after it succeeded (`payment_failed` on a PAID installment): the installment goes back to FAILED, what the vendor was paid for it comes back from their balance (less commission; an early deposit release is owed back and no longer taken off the final payout), the customer is asked to pay again and Vendly's admins are told.
- While a bank payment clears, a pending-payment window waiting for it is paused (no "Pending deposit", no deadline); if it fails, a fresh window starts then. The customer can't cancel (nor the vendor for "Customer didn't pay") until it clears: settling now would refund it in full when it lands. The vendor can still cancel for their own reasons (everything refunded).
- A return after a partial refund re-owes only what the customer kept (amount less the refund; the fee stays whole unless less is left). Rentals are cards only; another payment method picked for an add-on may be a bank account only on a large booking. A booking request whose payment method Stripe can't identify is refused (503, try again).
- After the job is done (custom plans' later payments), a failed payment gets the usual retries and "Payment problem", not a cancellation window.
- Payouts wait for cleared money: `releaseBooking` (and the hourly sweep) skips a booking while any bank payment is clearing; the early deposit release needs the deposit paid (cleared). Refunds go back through Stripe refunds and take a few days ("Refund on its way").
- Disputes: the partial refund a customer asks for is checked against what was actually paid (never more); the DTO bound is only a sanity limit ($1,000,000).

## Cancellation reasons

- `PATCH /bookings/:id/cancel` takes `reason_code` and an optional `note`. Customers: `plans_changed`, `found_another_vendor`, `event_cancelled`, `price`, `other`. Vendors: `customer_didnt_pay`, `safety_concern`, `emergency`, `double_booked`, `other` (`booking/cancel-reasons.ts`). The cancel preview returns the list to show as `reasons`. A reason from the other side's list is refused; apps from before the lists send none and count as `other` (the old free-text `reason` is kept as the note).
- Stored on the booking: `cancelled_by` (CUSTOMER | VENDOR | SYSTEM | ADMIN), `cancel_reason_code`, `cancel_reason` (the note), `cancelled_at`. Bookings return `cancel_reason_label`. The other side sees it on the booking and in the lists, admins in the booking details; the cancellation emails carry it, and so does the final invoice ("Cancelled by", "Cancellation reason").
- **Customer didn't pay** (vendors): only once a deposit plan's deadline to pay has passed (pending deposit/balance past `payment_due_by`), or a charge towards the price (`kind: 'charge'`) failed on other plans (full on accept, pay after service, bookings from before the deposit rules) after their retries. Failed add-ons, fees and rental deposit holds don't count. While a deposit plan's 12 hours are running the reason isn't offered and is refused with "The customer has until <time> to pay. If they don't, the booking is cancelled automatically." (time in the booking's time zone, as in the payment emails). It settles like a customer cancellation, not like the vendor's own. `GET /bookings/:id/cancel-preview?reason_code=customer_didnt_pay` previews that settlement.

## Vendor cancellation tracking

- Counted: a vendor's cancellations in the last 90 days whose reason isn't `customer_didnt_pay`, `safety_concern` or `non_payment` (older bookings without a reason count) (`booking/vendor-cancellations.ts`).
- At 2: Vendly's admins get one push and one email (`User.cancellations_alerted_at`; told again only once 90 days have passed since).
- Ranking: while a vendor has 2 or more, their packages come after everyone else's in marketplace search, in the same order otherwise. With database paging the page is filled from the other vendors' packages first and then the demoted ones (`MarketplaceService.searchListings`); for sorts done in memory (rating, distance) a stable sort moves them to the end. The list of demoted vendors is reread at most every 5 minutes.
- Admin vendor details show the count (`cancellations_90d`, `ranked_lower` on `GET /admin/user/vendors`).
- After a vendor's own cancellation (any reason but Customer didn't pay) the customer's email, push and booking page offer up to 4 packages in the same category from other active vendors free on the event day (`MarketplaceService.similarAvailable`, the marketplace date filter), with a link to `/marketplace?category_id=…&date=…`. The booking returns them as `similar_vendors`.

## Holding and releasing

- On completion the vendor is **not** paid immediately. `release_at` = completion + 3 days, or 48h after the vendor's last proof upload if sooner. A proof uploaded before completion counts from completion, so the customer always has at least 48h after completion to approve or report a problem.
- `POST /bookings/:id/approve` (customer) releases immediately.
- `POST /bookings/:id/dispute` (customer, before release) freezes the money until an admin resolves it (`dispute_resolved_at`; resolved from Admin → Disputes, below). Open once the booking is in progress or complete, or while it is still confirmed once its start time (`event_start_at`, else `scheduled_at`) has passed: the vendor may not have shown up. A partial refund asked for must be less than the vendor's part of what was paid, the most an admin can give short of a full refund.
- **Not started (hourly, under the money-check lock):** a confirmed booking (not a rental) still not started 2 hours after its start is flagged once (whole-hour windows): the vendor gets a push and email to start or contact the customer, admins a notification. Nothing moves. The timeline's `can_dispute` / `not_started` tell the apps to show Report a problem, and the cancel preview adds `report_problem_hint` for the customer (cancelling there goes by the policy, so the vendor would keep their share).
- An hourly cron releases due bookings: vendor share = paid installments − fee − refunds, sent as a Stripe Transfer to the vendor's Connect account; vendors without Connect get a Vendly balance credit instead (withdrawn through the existing `/withdraw` routes). Only installments actually paid and not yet paid out count. A booking with nothing paid (`paid_at` empty) isn't released or marked released: it waits, and is released by the sweep once the customer pays. The price is paid in one go only for Checkout-era bookings (no plan charges) whose checkout payment is on record. The sweep only picks paid bookings with no rented item still out, oldest release time first.
- Installments paid after the release (a failed balance paid later, add-ons) are paid out once by the late-payout sweep (`paid_out_at`).
- Customers can't cancel, and vendors can't give money back or release a deposit, while a dispute is open.
- Vendors can start work before the event (setting up), but can only mark it complete once the event (`event_start_at`, else `scheduled_at`) has started. Start and complete only move from the status they expect, so a cancellation that lands meanwhile is never overwritten.
- **Money check (hourly, report only):** released bookings that owed the vendor something but have no payout, earning or recovery record, and paid cancellations with neither a refund record nor the vendor's share settled, from one to two hours ago, are reported to Sentry and to admins (email and notification). Nothing is moved automatically.
- `GET /bookings/:id/payments` returns the timeline: plan, installments with status/attempts/errors, paid/outstanding, release and dispute state.

## Cancellations and refunds

- Tiers (`booking/cancellation.ts`) decide how much the vendor is owed; `PaymentsService.refundBooking` refunds newest installment first, voids uncaptured holds and cancels unpaid installments.
- On a payment plan the settlement is made against what was **actually paid** (`settleAgainstPaid`): a vendor cancelling refunds everything paid, Vendly Protection fee included; a customer cancelling leaves the vendor the smaller of what the policy owes them and what the customer paid towards the vendor price, and refunds the rest of that (the fee stays paid, `keepFees`). Unpaid installments are never charged. Add-ons approved after booking count as price under the policy; approved fees are the vendor's in full. A booking paid in one go through Checkout (no installments) is settled the same way, as one paid charge (price + fee) less what was refunded since (a dispute's partial refund, `checkoutPart`), so the preview and the cancellation never refund or credit more than is left.
- **Deposits are non-refundable** (deposit plans booked from Oct 2026, `deposit_rules`) when the customer cancels, when the vendor cancels with Customer didn't pay, and on a non-payment cancellation: the vendor keeps the paid deposit (its vendor part); of what was paid beyond it, the policy tier decides what comes back (`depositPlanVendorOwed`: deposit + the unrefunded part of the rest + fees agreed after booking, never more than was paid towards the vendor's price). Example: $1,000 on a 50% plan, all paid, cancelled in the 50% window: the vendor keeps $500 + $250, the customer gets $250 back. The Vendly Protection fee follows the customer cancellation rule (kept). The vendor's own cancellation still refunds everything. The preview and `refund_summary` carry `deposit_kept`, and the message says "Your deposit of $X is non-refundable."
- Every cancellation releases card holds (pay-after-service, rental deposits) and cancels add-on/fee requests still waiting (`voidOpenCharges`).
- Partial dispute refunds come out of the vendor's part (the fee goes back only with a full refund). On a booking paid in one go through Checkout (no plan charges) a dispute refund, full or partial, refunds the Checkout payment intent and records a `booking_refund`; the release afterwards pays the vendor only the price left after refunds. If the payment can't be found the decision is refused and nothing moves.
- If a cancellation refund fails part-way, only the part that didn't go back is recorded (`booking_refund`, status `refund_failed`) and reported to Sentry; the customer's email, push and `refund_summary` say how much is on its way (`refund_total`) and that Vendly will follow up on the rest (`refund_failed`).
- A duplicate or late Checkout payment is refunded; if that refund fails, Stripe's webhook retry tries it again (same idempotency key) while the row is still `refund_pending`.
- Payout maths: vendor share = Σ max(0, amount − fee − refunded) over paid installments, so an installment refunded past the vendor part (fee included) never reduces another one.
- Charges, captures, refunds and payouts on one booking take a Postgres advisory lock (`withBookingLock`), so they never act on the same installments at once. A charge is only claimed at the amount it read, so a lowered balance is never charged at its old price.

## Add-ons, fees and money back

- **Per person / hour / day packages** (`add-ons/pricing.ts`): price × guests (minimum/maximum enforced), × event hours in half hours (minimum charged, end time required), × days spanned. `VendorListing.min_quantity / max_quantity`.
- **Library** (`/vendor/add-ons`): `VendorAddOn` rows of kind `ADD_ON` or `FEE`, priced once / per person / per hour / each. Bulk attach to packages (`ListingAddOn`); custom add-ons (`in_library = false`) belong to one package and are deleted when removed from it. Up to 40 per package.
- **At booking:** customers pick a package's add-ons; they are priced into `Booking.amount` (promo applies to the total) and stored as `BookingExtra` rows with status `INCLUDED`. The contract gets a **Schedule A** with how the price was reached, the add-ons, and the package's fees, stating that any fee or later add-on needs the customer's approval. Amendments carry Schedule A over.
- **After booking** (`BookingExtrasService`): the vendor sends an add-on (from confirmation) or a fee (from the start of the job until 14 days after completion), with a reason. The customer approves (charged on the booking card or another saved card as an installment of `kind = extra`; Vendly Protection % on add-ons, none on fees) or declines with a reason. One reminder after 24 h. A declined fee, or one unanswered for 72 h, can be sent to Vendly review: Admin → Fee reviews charges or waives it with a note; both sides are told. Up to 10 open requests and $25,000 per request.
- **Extras paid after the payout** are paid out on their own at the booking's commission rate (`payOutLateInstallments`, hourly, and straight after a payment). `BookingInstallment.paid_out_at` records what has been paid out.
- **Give money back** (`POST /bookings/:id/give-back`, vendor): first lowers what the customer still owes (scheduled or failed charges, uncaptured pay-after-service holds — the hold is then captured for the lower amount), then refunds the vendor's part of what was paid. The Vendly Protection fee is unchanged. If the vendor was already paid for that money, it is taken back from their balance less the commission Vendly took on it (`Booking.released_gross` holds the commission base). Money Vendly still holds is refunded first, and paid-out money only as far as the vendor's current balance covers taking it back; beyond that the vendor is told to wait until the balance covers it or contact support, so Vendly never funds the refund. Shown to the customer as `given_back` on `/bookings/:id/payments`.

## Admin tools (Phase C)

- **Fees live in the database** (`platform_settings`), edited at Admin → Settings: Vendly Protection fee %, minimum fee, vendor commission %, currency/limits. Per-category fee overrides (`ServiceCategory.fee_percent`). Cached in memory (`currentFees()`) and refreshed on every change; env values are the fallback. The old JSON-file settings store (wiped on each Railway deploy) is gone.
- **Vendor commission** is taken from the vendor share at release and recorded as `vendly_commission` (default 0%).
- **Disputes:** Admin → Disputes lists open disputes with the customer's report and what is held; decide release / partial refund / full refund with a note. Both sides are emailed. A full refund claws back any early-released deposit. On a booking not complete yet (a reported no-show): a full refund of a booking never started also cancels it (and, as every cancellation, calls off what is still to charge and releases holds, `voidOpenCharges`); release or a partial refund leaves the rest held until the vendor marks it complete (the usual release clock then).
- **Early deposit release:** `POST /bookings/:id/release-deposit` (vendor, `DEPOSIT_50`/`DEPOSIT_25`/`DEPOSIT_CUSTOM` bookings whose first plan charge is paid and whose balance is still to come, contract executed, Stripe Connect set up). Subtracted from the final payout. A vendor who took the deposit early cancels through Vendly (support), and so does a customer whose refund would reach into that deposit (older bookings without the deposit rules). When Vendly refunds money already paid out (a full-refund dispute, a returned bank payment), it is first taken back from the vendor's Stripe account (transfer reversal, `payout_reversal`); what can't be (already at their bank) makes the vendor's Vendly balance negative, recovered from the next payouts first.
- **Promo codes:** Admin → Promo codes. Percent or fixed amount off the vendor price, optional minimum, max uses and expiry. Customers enter a code on the booking request (web modal and mobile summary); the preview shows the discount and the code is redeemed atomically when the booking is created. `POST /promo/check` validates a code.
- **Feature flags:** Admin → Settings. `GET /flags` (public) returns enabled keys.
- **Revenue report:** Admin → Revenue: collected, Vendly fees, commission, refunds and payouts by day, with CSV export.
- **Suspend / restore:** Admin → Vendors, `PATCH /admin/users/:id/status`. Suspended users are rejected on their next request.
- **Withdrawals** (`POST /withdraw/request`, minimum $20): the amount is taken off the Vendly balance first, only if it is still there, then sent as a transfer in whole cents keyed on the pending `withdraw` record (transfer group `withdraw_<id>`, metadata `withdrawal_id`). A transfer Stripe refuses puts the money back (`failed`); one Stripe didn't answer is tried once more with the same key, and if still unknown stays off the balance as `needs_review` (Sentry) until Vendly checks it.
- **Withdrawals to check:** hourly (job lock `withdraw-review`), each `needs_review` withdrawal over an hour old is looked up in Stripe by its group (older ones: the only plain transfer of that amount to the vendor's account within an hour of the request). Found: `completed` with the transfer id. None: the money goes back to the balance and it is `failed`. More than one candidate, or Stripe not answering: left for an admin. Admin → Transactions lists them (`GET /admin/withdrawals/needs-review`) with Mark completed / Return to balance (`POST /admin/withdrawals/:id/settle` `{ outcome: completed|returned, transfer_id? }`). Settling claims the `needs_review` status, so the check and an admin never both act; the vendor gets a push either way.
- **Wallet:** the website no longer offers Add Funds (nothing in Vendly spends a customer balance); the deposit endpoints are unchanged.
- **Subscriptions:** the webhooks find the vendor's plan by `stripe_subscription_id`. Cancelling changes nothing (and returns an error) if Stripe couldn't cancel the subscription.

## Rentals (Phase D)

- Rental bookings (`POST /rentals/book`) have no package: `listing_id` is null and the booking holds one `RentalReservation` per item. Price = daily rate × days × quantity, charged in full when the vendor accepts (the usual engine). The Vendly Protection fee uses the first item's category.
- **Security deposit:** Σ(deposit per unit × quantity) is stored on `Booking.security_deposit` and scheduled as an installment with `kind = security_deposit`, `capture_manually = true`, due the day before pickup. Completion never captures it.
- **Return** (`POST /rentals/bookings/:id/return`, vendor): no fees voids the hold; damage / late fees are captured from the hold (partial capture), and anything above it becomes a PENDING fee (`BookingExtra`, kind FEE) the customer approves or declines like any other fee; nothing above the hold is charged without approval. Fees need photos or a note and are at most $25,000 per return; the customer gets 48h to dispute what was kept from the hold before payout.
- Payouts wait until every reservation on the booking is returned.
- Card holds lapse after about 7 days, so a deposit on a rental longer than ~6 days may expire before the return. If capture fails the fees are charged to the saved card instead (and can fail like any charge).

## Contract amendments and money

- A price amendment re-prices the booking's unpaid charges, keeping their rows and due dates (`rebasedAmounts`): one charge takes price + fee; a deposit plan splits it like at accept. The fee uses the package's category rate. It is only possible while no plan charge has been taken or started; after that it is refused ("use Give money back to lower it; add an add-on to charge more"). Checked at preview, request and signing.
- A date amendment moves the unpaid charges that follow the event (`eventLinkedDueAt`): a deposit plan's balance (7 or 14 days before), the pay-after-service charge (at the event), and a rental deposit hold (the day before). Never into the past.

## Account deletion

- Refused while the user (as customer or vendor) has bookings that are pending, confirmed or in progress, completed but not settled (money taken or still due and the vendor not paid out), or with an open dispute; while their Vendly balance isn't zero (money in it: withdraw first or contact support; money owed: contact support); or while a withdrawal is pending or being checked. The message lists what is open and what to do; ID check photos are only removed once deletion goes ahead.

## What people see about their money

- **Cancel preview:** `GET /bookings/:id/cancel-preview` (customer or vendor) returns what cancelling now would do: `{ cancelled_by, refund_total, refund_fee, fee_kept, vendor_keeps, policy: {key,label,text}|null, percent, unpaid_cancelled, holds_released, card, refund_timing, message }`. It runs the same `BookingService.cancellationTerms` as the real cancel (which returns the same shape as `refund_summary`), so the two never differ. `message` is a plain sentence for whoever asked ("You'll get $225.00 back to your card. The $22.50 Vendly Protection fee isn't refunded.").
- **After a cancellation** the customer always gets an email and push with the refund amount, the card and "5–10 business days", whoever cancelled; the vendor is told when the customer cancels. `GET /bookings/:id/payments` carries `refunded`, `refund_card`, `refund_timing` and `invoice_available` for the Refund card on the booking.
- **Receipts:** every installment that becomes `PAID` (deposit, balance, pay-after capture, add-on or fee, rental deposit kept) emails the customer a receipt once (`BookingInstallment.receipt_sent_at`, claimed first; a mail failure never touches the payment). An hourly sweep sends receipts missed in the last 7 days.
- **Final invoice:** `GET /bookings/:id/invoice` (customer, vendor or admin) returns a PDF: package (from Schedule A), add-ons at booking and later, promo, fees, Vendly Protection fee, every charge with status, refunds, money given back and the total paid; the vendor's and admin's copy add the payout and commission. `GET /bookings/:id/invoice/link` returns a five-minute signed link (`/api/invoices/:token`) the mobile app opens without a login header. The customer is emailed once (`Booking.invoice_sent_at`) when the money is final: the vendor is paid out, or a paid booking's cancellation is settled (and by the hourly sweep).
- **Transaction history:** `GET /payments/transactions?type=all|payment|refund|payout|fee&from&to&q&page&limit&role=customer|vendor`. Customers: charges (−) and refunds (+) from installments, plus refunds of Checkout-era bookings. Vendors: their part of each customer payment (pending until released), refunds of it, payouts, balance credits, early deposits, withdrawals, commission, money given back and recoveries. `q` searches the booking title. Transactions created from now on carry the booking in `order_id`.
- **Checkout-era bookings** paid after completion are credited to the vendor balance less the current Vendly commission (recorded as `vendly_commission`, with `commission_amount` / `released_gross` / `released_at` set), like a regular payout. Credits made before this change are untouched.
